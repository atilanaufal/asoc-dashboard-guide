Mathematical Formulation

Risk Score Calculation & Formulas

This section details the mathematical modeling governing the Risk Score metric across the ASOC Client Dashboard, detailing individual machine weighting through to the aggregated institutional exposure score.

1). Tenant Risk Score Formula

The overall Tenant Risk Score represents the normalized average of the accumulated risk values across all monitored endpoint agents registered under that tenant:

Tenant Risk Score = (AS₁ + AS₂ + ... + ASₙ) / Total Registered Agents
  • Where AS₁, AS₂, ... ASₙ denotes the computed individual risk rating for each endpoint (Agent Score).
  • Total Registered Agents is the aggregate count of all Wazuh agents provisioned under the tenant (encompassing both online and offline machines).

2). Device / Agent Score (AS) Formula

Each individual machine is evaluated based on the accumulated severity weights of all security alerts detected on that endpoint throughout the active observation interval:

Agent Score (AS) = Min( 100, Σ (Severity Weight × Alert Count) )
  • The maximum Agent Score is constrained at an upper ceiling of 100 (capped at 100).

3). Severity Weight Matrix

Incident weighting maps directly to Wazuh SIEM rule severity levels:

Wazuh Rule Level Severity Tier Score Weight Representative Attack Samples
Level 15+ Critical +6 Ransomware Encryption, EICAR Malware, Web Defacement, Rootkit Execution
Level 12 - 14 High +3 Successful SSH Brute Force, SQL Injection, Unauthorized Privilege Escalation
Level 7 - 11 Medium +1 Repeated Failed Logins, Nmap Port Scanning, Suspicious Binary Download

4). Risk Level Category Classification

The calculated Tenant Risk Score maps into 5 distinct operational risk tiers:

Score Range Risk Category Color Indicator Operational Significance
0 - 20 Low Green Infrastructure secure and stable. Only routine, minor anomalies detected.
21 - 40 Moderate Orange Isolated recurring suspicious activities requiring monitoring.
41 - 60 Elevated Dark Orange Targeted attack campaigns detected against key network hosts.
61 - 80 High Red Active high-severity cyber threats actively underway.
81 - 100 Critical Dark Red Emergency security condition. Widespread attack or critical asset compromise.

5). Step-by-Step Calculation Simulation

Consider an institution monitoring 4 registered endpoint agents:

  • Host A (Academic Database Server): Triggers 5 Critical alerts (5 × 6 = 30), 10 High alerts (10 × 3 = 30), and 15 Medium alerts (15 × 1 = 15).
    Accumulation AS(A) = 30 + 30 + 15 = 75 (Classification: High).
  • Host B (Public Web Portal): Experiences aggressive brute force with 20 Critical alerts (20 × 6 = 120).
    Accumulation AS(B) = min(100, 120) = 100 (Capped at maximum ceiling 100, Classification: Critical).
  • Host C (Library Catalog Host): Triggers 5 Medium alerts (5 × 1 = 5).
    Accumulation AS(C) = 5 (Classification: Low).
  • Host D (Staff Workstation): Clean baseline with no detected alerts.
    Accumulation AS(D) = 0 (Classification: Low).

Final Tenant Score Computation:

Total Agent Score = 75 + 100 + 5 + 0 = 180
Tenant Risk Score = 180 / 4 = 45.0

The resulting institutional risk score evaluates to 45.0, classifying the environment under the Elevated risk tier. This informs administrators that immediate remediation should focus heavily on Host A and Host B.
